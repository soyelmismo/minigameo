## HOW TO PROVE SOMETHING IS DEAD (not "looks unused")

Grep the whole repo including scenes and tests, not just `.gd` files:

    grep -rn "the_symbol" src/ tests/ --include=*.gd
    grep -rn "the_symbol" src/ tests/ --include=*.tscn --include=*.tres

A symbol referenced only from a `.tscn` or a `.tres` is NOT dead. A symbol whose
only caller is itself, or a test that only exercises the symbol, is a judgement
call: prefer pinning it with a test over deleting it (the repo already chose that
policy for four state readers, see `tests/unit/test_archetype_state_readers.gd`).
