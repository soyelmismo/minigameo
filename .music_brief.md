## Mission

The music must behave like a Nintendo game: calm in freeroam, rising with the
combo, euphoric inside a minigame, and resolving cleanly. Today there are six
stems and a MusicDirector; verify they actually do that and fix what does not.

1. **Layering by intensity** — map the match state and score/energy to a clear
   intensity ladder. The stems must enter and leave by crossfade, never by a
   hard cut, and never by restarting a layer that is already playing. Verify
   each transition by driving the director directly and asserting the state.
2. **Silence discipline** — a layer that is not meant to play must not be
   playing: a stem left running after its state ends is a bug. Check the
   `play`/`stop` bookkeeping, including when a minigame ends and the style
   reverts.
3. **Minigame states** — `MinigameMetadata.music_state` is already read by the
   director chain; verify the minigame music state actually reaches
   MusicDirector and that the base track resumes afterwards.
4. **Sound environment** — the ambience loop must sit under everything without
   masking it: verify levels against the bus layout, and that ducking (VO and
   impacts) is audible but not excessive.
5. **Assets** — if a stem or SFX is unsatisfactory, regenerate it with
   `tools/audio/build_audio.py` (numpy). Everything you generate must stay
   inside the format the importer already uses so the `.import` sidecars keep
   working. Deterministic builds only.
