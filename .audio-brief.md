## WHAT IS ALREADY DONE — BUILD ON IT, DO NOT REDO IT

- `MusicDirector` is fully implemented: a locked BPM beat grid
  (`music_bpm` in GameConfig), four vertical layers (drums/bass/lead/harmony-fx)
  with gate-based intensity, bar-locked state switching, a pre-mixed fallback
  bed, and silence discipline (a faded-out stem is stopped, not left at -80 dB).
- `Gain ramps now use a CONSTANT dB/second over the anchored span`. This was a
  real bug (see below) — do not reintroduce a proportional ramp.

  DEFECT I JUST FIXED, so you know the shape of it: all three ramps computed
  `step = |target - current| / fade_s * delta`. That is asymptotic — it closes a
  fixed fraction of the remaining distance every frame and never lands. A duck
  to -6 dB sat at -5.19 dB after the full fade and kept creeping forever. The
  duck also derived its rate from the target's magnitude, so releasing to 0 dB
  had a rate of ZERO and the music stayed quiet for seconds after the VO ended.
  The ramp is now anchored (`_duck_from`) and constant-rate.
- `play_impact()` on SFXPlayer is new and deliberate: an impact frame carries a
  bare `AudioStream`, not a catalog id. It reuses the voice pool, the stealing
  policy and the finished() bookkeeping. Its bus/group/priority/volume are read
  from GameConfig (`impact_sfx_bus`, `impact_sfx_priority`, `impact_sfx_group`,
  `impact_sfx_volume_db`) — those are DATA, do not hardcode them.
- 10 real announcer VO lines exist in `assets/audio/voice/`, built by
  `tools/audio/build_voice.py`. `VoiceDirector` resolves them by event id.
- The `Impact` bus exists in `BusLayout` and is created by `setup_buses()`.
